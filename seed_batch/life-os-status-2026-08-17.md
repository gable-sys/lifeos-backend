## 2. Diet — Targets & Principles

### Standing targets
- **Protein: ~0.7g/lb bodyweight.**
- **Front-load protein.** Under-eating — protein especially backloaded — is the single most consistent and costly pattern. Eggs before workouts, yogurt bowls as add-ons, not as the meal.
- Carbs are supported, not restricted. Rice is a green light — thyroid/hormone support, glycogen replacement.
- Potassium over sodium (facial puffiness).
- Anti-seed-oil. Olive oil (Cobram Estate / California Olive Ranch) as default fat.
- Dairy emphasis, morning light, frequent eating (Ray Peat framework, selectively applied).
- Rejected: collagen powder, "detox" products, olive-oil-and-lemon shots, extreme juice protocols, anti-omega-3 position, thyroid self-experimentation.

### Looksmaxxing-specific food levers
- Omega-3 for skin: arctic char (best mild salmon substitute), trout, sablefish. **Sardines rejected.**
- Zinc: oysters, pumpkin seeds
- Iron: red meat, lentils, spinach
- Beta-carotene veg (within the 15mg cap)
- Dark chocolate ~1 oz/day max. Criteria in priority order: (1) low heavy metals — cadmium/lead, the biggest under-checked issue; (2) 80–85% cocoa sweet spot; (3) non-alkalized/non-Dutched — "processed with alkali" destroys 60–90% of flavanols; (4) ≤4 ingredients; (5) real cane sugar over sugar alcohols; (6) origin transparency. Taza = clean default. Current in-stock: Endangered Species 72%.

### Cooking setup
- **Single burner in both locations.** All recipes must work sequentially in one pan.
- Standard bowl template: protein crusted hard → beans blistered with cumin + smoked paprika → veg steamed under a lid → spinach wilted off-heat → tahini-yogurt-lemon sauce → kimchi raw on the side (never cooked, preserves probiotics).
- Basmati or jasmine rice as preferred grain.
- Ajitsuke tamago (soy-marinated jammy eggs) now in rotation for batch prep: 6.5 min boil, ice bath, peel, marinate 4hr–overnight in 3 tbsp soy / 3 tbsp water / 1 tbsp honey. Holds 3–4 days.
- **Every component gets its seasoning stated explicitly — not just the protein.**

## 5. Training

- Recent session: sprints, abs, chest.
- Session order that works: eggs ~45 min before → sprints → chest → abs.
- Sprint protocol: 10 min easy warm-up + 2 build-ups at 70–80% before going full (cold sprinting is the hamstring risk), then 6–8 × 20–30 sec hard, 90 sec rest. Stop when times drop off, not at a rep count.
