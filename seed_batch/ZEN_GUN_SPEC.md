## PROTOCOLS / ROUTINES

### PM skincare/hair (nightly)
1. Cleanse → **dry 10–15 min**
2. Rice milk toner
3. Tretinoin (tret nights only — every other night): one pea face, one–two peas scalp (hairline, temples, crown). Keep away from eyes.
4. Moisturizer
5. Minoxidil **20–30 min after tret** (tret first, wait, then minox). Minox is nightly regardless of tret night.
6. Castor oil on lashes (disposable wand)
7. Mouth tape
- Off-tret nights: self-tanner per Sun/Tue/Thu cadence. Exfoliant max ~1x/week, only non-tret non-recovery nights. Derma roller (if used) non-tret nights only, never stacked with tret.

### AM routine
- Vitamin C serum → SPF. Morning toner slot empty on regular days. Gua sha date days only (post-toner, before serum). Finasteride + vitamin D + zinc taken in the morning.

### Hair styling
Towel pat dry → sea salt spray on damp hair → blow dry medium heat (flip head, finger-forward for volume) → cold shot → light volume powder at roots → style with fingers only → paperboy hat to shape while slightly damp. Off-center part sweeping forward to minimize temple recession. **No comb after volume powder.** Go Blonder spray on damp hair before styling when lightening.

### Standard meal template ("the bowl")
Protein (crusted hard) + beans (blistered with cumin/smoked paprika) + layered veg in cook-time order + eggs (pulled at ~80% with turmeric + black pepper) + tahini-yogurt-lemon(-garlic-honey) sauce + raw olive oil drizzle + kimchi cold on the side. Variation: sesame-honey-kimchi pan glaze.
- Technique notes: chicken breast sliced thin, pulled at 80%; chicken thighs whole 5–6 min/side; chickpeas need an extra blister minute (dry well, oil, salt/pepper/turmeric/red pepper flakes, 4–5 min); cannellini fold gently (rinse-only if home-pressure-cooked); salmon skin-side down ~4 min then flip 2–3 min.
