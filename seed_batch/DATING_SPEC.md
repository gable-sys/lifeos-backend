## RULES & PREFERENCES

**Communication register (critical for any advisor):**
- "Treat me like a bro": direct, tactical, honest. Give the move, not just the observation.
- Name a pattern ONCE per session, then move on. Do not relitigate, moralize, repeat, soften excessively, or get preachy — he pushes back hard on all of these.
- He communicates in lowercase stream-of-consciousness voice dumps, often mid-thought. Processes by re-talking through material.
- He loses track of dates constantly. ASK for explicit day confirmation; never infer the timeline from context. Repeated, flagged friction point.
- Suggested text drafts must sound like real texting (lowercase, casual). He has rejected stiff/unnatural lines ("you suck at flirting").
- Prefers audiobooks over reading.
- Do not treat normal sibling communication with Gara as strategic risk (previously overcorrected on this).
- He has consistently declined professional support suggestions; prefers action over processing. Don't re-pitch it every session.

**Dating rules (settled):**
- Ellen's texting speed carries no signal. Do not let him extract meaning from response latency. Behavioral evidence is the scoreboard that counts.
- Wayne's no-contact / "go dark" tactics do NOT apply to Ellen's situation (balanced cadence, confirmed plans, escalating investment). Manufactured silence before a locked date is a misapplication.
- The only green-lit Betty move is one made from a calm, flat state — never from an activated/spiked evening. Trigger for outreach is a STATE (calm, outcome-indifferent, not mid-spiral), not a date on the calendar.
- Every new Betty framing (glow-up reveal, urgency, strategic timing, logistics pretext) is the same emotional spike in new clothes. Name it once, decline it.
- Rekindling research (settled): 30-day no-contact rule is pop psychology with thin empirical basis; rekindled relationships trend lower quality; highest-risk pattern is reuniting when nothing structural has changed. The Betty question is a longer-arc variable, not a 60-day project.
- Sulking is a covert bid for attention, not genuine withdrawal. Frameworks used as armor against vulnerability ≠ non-neediness.
- Post-date loneliness is structural to undefined dating, not a failure signal.
- Sliver of separation: touch/attention with space, receiving without immediately reciprocating — applies to texting, conversation, and physical intimacy.
- Upper-class dating operates via social circulation, not visible effort: be the node, not the applicant. A release show (Sunny's, Barbès) creates legitimate ambient reentry points without direct outreach.

**Known patterns to watch (name once, redirect):**
- **The scoreboard loop**: low state → scoreboard checking → neutral signals read as rejection → reassurance-seeking → temporary resolution → regenerates within hours with new surface content (response timing, looks anxiety, sulking regret, withdrawal urges). Content changes; loop persists.
- **The Betty spike**: see above.
- **Anticipation as drink trigger**: high-stakes emotional anticipation, esp. Betty-related. Coverage needed for the window AFTER the event, not just before.
- Underlying thread (flagged, not rejected, not committed to): longstanding body image + performance anxiety runs beneath the scoreboard, drink triggers, and compliment-counting.
