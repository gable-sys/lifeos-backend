## GOALS (in priority order)
1. **Shoulder width** — side/medial delts. Top priority. Lateral raises are the driver.
2. **V-taper** — wide lats (pull-ups) + tight waist (abs). It's a ratio: widen the top, keep the middle tight.
3. **Bigger / wider chest** — dips (park) + DB floor press & flyes (home).
4. **Visible abs** — trained often, but mostly revealed by staying lean (diet, not just crunches).
5. **Legs + calves** — chronically neglected; needs real attention. Sprints count as leg work.
6. **Neck** — mostly a natural result; light isometric holds if convenient.
- **Arms are already a strong point** — maintenance only. NO dedicated arm block needed. They get plenty from pull-ups, chins, dips.

- **Track/outdoor:** sprints.

## STYLE & PREFERENCES
- **Calisthenics-forward, high-rep "spam" style** (follows Maximilian on YouTube). Minimalist. Dumbbells mainly for delts (laterals) plus a couple of moves — not a dumbbell-heavy program.
- **Lateral raises near-daily** — low fatigue, drives width. Rotate: straight / lean-away / burnout + partials.
- **Light dumbbells (15 lb):** buy back the difficulty with higher reps, slow tempo, pauses, and single-leg/single-arm versions. Take sets close to failure.
- **Dead hang + support hold** most days (decompression, grip, shoulder health).
- **Warm-up ritual:** light bounce/lymph + deep breathing, then shoulder pass-throughs, arm circles, leg swings.
- **Supersets** to save time (pair opposites: pull-ups + dips, floor press + laterals). Big lifts straight-set and focused; accessories paired and fast.
- **Order:** bodyweight/park work first, dumbbell work last (often done at home after the park).
- Likes ~5 training days/week, dislikes stacking rest days but will rest when genuinely run-down.
- **Progressive overload + logging** — beat last session's reps, then add load (backpack on pull-ups, etc.). He has a rep tracker.

## RULES & CAUTIONS (important)
- **ROTATOR CUFF:** history of shoulder soreness + sharp pain in the top/outer corner (especially on rear delt flyes). → Keep dips at MODERATE depth, never two hard dip days back-to-back, go LIGHT or skip rear-delt raises if they flare, warm up the shoulder, and STOP any movement that pinches. Two flags = take shoulder-rest days when it nags.
- **QUAD DOMS:** ramps leg volume too fast and gets wrecked. Ease into Bulgarian split squats & lunges — moderate, not to failure, especially the first session back.
- **Sprints = leg work** (great for hamstrings/glutes/quads). ~1–2x/week. Don't stack all-out sprints on top of a hard lift when fatigued; if the lift is the priority, sprints go last; if sprints are the focus, do them fresh.
- **Cap hard conditioning ~2x/week** so it doesn't burn the surplus he needs to grow.
- **Push then pull balance:** don't do dips + push-ups with no pulling. Pair opposites.

## NUTRITION (the #1 lever — he's a hard-gainer)
- Must eat in a **calorie surplus** with **~160g protein/day**.
- **Appetite drops after training** — he needs to eat anyway, especially post-workout.
- Food + consistency over 8–12 weeks is what actually produces the width/chest/size — more than any exercise swap. Keep reminding him gently.
- Currently coming off a minor illness + a weekend of drinking → recovery lagging a few days; keep sessions submaximal and hydrate until back to 100%.
