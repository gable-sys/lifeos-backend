🚶 Zone 2 — 25–30 min
Heart rate 60–70% max. Conversational the whole time. Pick one or rotate.
A.Marching in place — knees high, arms pumping, steady rhythm.
B.Step-ups on a chair — continuous, alternate leading leg every minute.
C.Shadow boxing — light continuous movement, feet moving, hands up.
D.Slow burpees — deliberate pace. If you can't talk, slow down.
💡Speak a full sentence out loud to check. Can't talk = too hard. Effortless = push slightly more.

📌 Week 4 Rules
Arms — curls on pull days (Tue + Fri), triceps on push days (Mon + Wed). This is the frequency that builds arms fast.
Chair dips — lean slightly forward for chest emphasis. 90° elbow depth. No lower or you stress the shoulder joint.
Daily pull-ups and push-ups — spread throughout the day on and off. Never to complete failure. Leave 1-2 reps in the tank each set so you can keep doing them all day.
Lateral raises — lead with pinky, stop at shoulder height. Above that recruits traps not delts.
Neck holds every session — 2 rounds, 15 sec each direction. Trap-neutral. 2 min total.
Burpee intervals — 20 sec max, 40 sec full rest. Walk the recovery. That's where testosterone spikes.
