The Serious Player's Curriculum — Jazz, Gypsy & Flatpicking

Your Guitar Practice
Master Schedule

30–45 min/day · Jazz · Gypsy Jazz · Flatpicking · Bluegrass · Music Theory
🎸

Core principle: Every session has ONE primary focus and ONE secondary. Jazz harmony is woven into most days because it's the connective tissue between all your goals. Theory isn't studied in isolation — it's applied directly to the guitar neck, chord voicings, and solos you're already learning.

I
Daily Session Structure (30–45 min)
5min
warmup
Warmup

Chromatic Exercises + Stretch

Spider exercises across all 6 strings, slow chromatic runs. Never skip — protects your hands and primes your ear. Use a metronome at 60 bpm.

15min
primary
Jazz / Gypsy

Main Focus Block

Deep work on the day's primary topic (see weekly schedule). One concept at a time. If it's a chord voicing, learn it in 3 keys minimum. If it's a lick, slow it down 50% first.

10min
secondary
Flatpick / Theory

Secondary Skill Block

Alternate between flatpicking technique (alternating pick strokes, crosspicking) and applied theory. Theory always maps to something physical — scales on the neck, not just paper.

5–15min
play
Freeplay

Apply + Enjoy

Jam over a backing track (iReal Pro is essential). Use whatever you worked on today. No stopping to fix mistakes — just flow. This is where it all becomes music.

IV
Progress Milestones
Phase 1: Foundation
Weeks 1–4

Build the Jazz Vocabulary Base

Know all shell chords (3rds & 7ths) for major, minor, dominant. Understand ii-V-I in 3 keys. Learn la pompe rhythm for gypsy. Know 2 fiddle tunes for flatpicking. Complete Fretboard Logic Vol. 1.

Weeks 5–8

Expand Drop-2 Voicings + Bebop Entry

Drop-2 chords on all string sets. First bebop scale (dominant bebop). 3 gypsy jazz standards (Minor Swing, Nuages, All of Me). Crosspicking clean at 80 bpm. Modes learned positionally on neck.

Phase 2: Integration
Months 3–4

Chord-Scale Fluency + Repertoire

5+ jazz standards you can play rhythm and solo over. Flatpicking up to 100 bpm with accuracy. Guide tone soloing over changes. Begin reading Mark Levine. Record yourself weekly.

Months 5–6

Stylistic Confidence

Sit in with others or jam online. Play a Django tune start-to-finish with rhythm. Bluegrass rhythm behind a singer. Your theory understanding maps to improvisation — you know where to go and why.
